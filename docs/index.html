Your own music,
on every device.

A music player for the files you own, on your computer and your phone. Synced through your own NAS. No streaming, no ads, no account.

Download for Windows Download for Android
Free and open source (MIT). Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · Android 8 or newer.

Everything your music needs, and nothing it doesn't

Melue plays MP3, FLAC, AAC, ALAC, Ogg Vorbis and WAV files from folders on your computer or phone. Keep your library on a network share and every device gets the same songs, playlists, likes and settings.

Your library, everywhere

Songs, albums, artists and folders with covers. Home brings back what you played last; "Made for you" follows your likes and most played songs.

Synced through your own share

Songs are copied to each device, so they play offline. Playlists, likes, play counts and settings stay the same everywhere. A phone can wait for Wi-Fi.

Your devices work together

Continue on your computer where your phone stopped, or play your phone's queue on your computer's speakers with the phone as the remote.

Timed lyrics

Lyrics follow the song, word by word where they can: from a .lrc file, the song's tags, or LRCLIB if you allow it.

Spotify playlists, your songs

Paste a public Spotify playlist's link: Melue matches its songs to yours and follows it as it changes. No Spotify account or Premium.

Made for both

The keyboard everywhere on a computer; Android's media controls and Android Auto on a phone; a dark and a light theme; updates that install themselves.

Questions

Does Melue stream music?

No. It plays files you have. Spotify playlists are matched to your own songs; Melue never plays anything from Spotify.

Can I use Melue without syncing anything?

Yes. Without a share, Melue is a music player for the folders on that device: nothing leaves it, and nothing else is needed.

Do I need to keep my NAS or shared folder online?

Only for syncing. Each device keeps its own copy of the songs and plays them offline. A phone syncs when Melue is open and can reach the share, at home say; away from home it can sync over mobile data through a VPN to your home network, within what the mobile data setting allows. Continuing on another device and Connect device need both devices online too.

What happens when I add or remove a song?

Put it in your share's music folder, or a music folder on a computer: Melue picks it up. Easier still, drop the files on Melue on a computer: it copies them into the right place by their tags (Artist/Album/), on your share when you have one, and on a playlist's page into that playlist too. A song removed from the share is kept on your devices or removed there too (a setting).

Does Melue need Spotify Premium or a Spotify account?

No. A playlist only needs to be public: paste its link and Melue reads it like a web browser would, with no sign-in.

What if a song from a Spotify playlist isn't in my library?

It stays in the playlist, greyed out, so you can see what's missing, and the list of missing songs can be copied. When you add the song later, it fills in by itself.

What goes online?

Only lyrics from LRCLIB if you switch it on, Spotify playlists whose link you paste, album covers for your Discord status if you connect Discord, and checking GitHub for a new version. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Is there a version for macOS, Linux or iPhone?

Not as a download. Melue is built with Tauri, so macOS and Linux builds from source should work, but they aren't tested yet; there's no iPhone version.
